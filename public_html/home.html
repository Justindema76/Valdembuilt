Larissa DeMatteis

principal & founder

Larissa De Matteis brings years of expertise in architectural and interior design, delivering exceptional results across a variety of scales and settings. Her career began at a boutique architecture firm, where she refined her skills in design, project management, and consultant coordination. During this time, she contributed to the creation and construction of stunning hotels, spas, restaurants, and event venues, gaining invaluable experience in the hospitality and commercial sectors.

Larissa later expanded her expertise into residential design and building, embracing a hands-on role as a site supervisor. This practical experience provided her with a deep understanding of what it takes to create functional, well-crafted spaces from the ground up.

While she still values the occasional on-site visit, Larissa has shifted her focus to what she loves most: the art and craft of design. She takes immense pride in guiding her clients through the creative process, sharing valuable insights and best practices to ensure their spaces are not only beautiful but also enduring. Larissa’s technical background equips her to collaborate seamlessly with architects, contractors, and other stakeholders, helping to transform her clients’ renovation dreams into reality. Whether managing hospitality and commercial projects or designing bespoke residential spaces, she consistently balances creativity with practicality, always mindful of budget and timelines. Her passion for design and commitment to excellence continue to define her work, making her a trusted partner for clients looking to create spaces that inspire and endure.
